# CIS web studios / web production shortlist

A pragmatic shortlist of **50 notable web-focused firms** across CIS-adjacent markets, emphasizing web studios, web production companies, product/web shops, and design+web specialists over broad full-cycle digital agencies where possible.

## Coverage
- **Kazakhstan:** 11
- **Ukraine:** 10
- **Belarus:** 8
- **Armenia:** 9
- **Georgia:** 5
- **Uzbekistan:** 5
- **Total:** 48 verified/pragmatic entries in final JSON (started from ~50 target; kept only reasonably relevant names)

## Strongest signals found on official sites
- **MEGASITE (Ukraine):** 15+ years, 940+ projects.
- **ASTUDIO (Armenia):** web-centric service breadth; 5-year warranty; search sources cite 700+ projects.
- **Digital Brains (Armenia):** 250+ projects, 6+ years, visible case list.
- **UPQODE (Kazakhstan / intl.):** 40+ awards, visible portfolio.

## Notes / methodology
- Used web search for market discovery and official sites when fetchable for proof signals.
- Some official sites were unreachable from the environment (`fetch failed`) or protected; for those, I preserved entries only when they were repeatedly visible in rankings/search summaries.
- “Top” here means **visible / reputable / notable**, not authoritative ranking truth.
- A few entries, especially in **Uzbekistan** and **Georgia**, are more pragmatic due to weaker third-party ranking coverage in the available tools.
- I excluded many giant full-cycle outsourcing firms unless they had a meaningful web/product-studio identity.
